# W10. Utility 01
In this class, we will use the concept of the Markov Blanket introduced last class to expand our understanding of utility beyond singular agents, to groups, swarms, and colonies. We will see why a Markov Blanket is a useful analytical tool at different scales of analysis, and how this concept can be understood using the 4 E's of cognition.

## Pre-readings and Videos


### Active InferAnts
This paper uses the framework of Bayesian inference to analyze the cognition of ant colonies. This model is powerful because it can account for differing functionality of colony members, as well as understanding the colony at different hierarchical scales. [Active InferAnts](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/behavioral-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnbeh.2021.647732/full)
