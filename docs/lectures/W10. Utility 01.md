# W10. Utility 01
In our last week of class, we will expand our understanding of emergence by integrating something essential to natural life: goal-directed behaviour. Goal-directed behaviour allows a system to model and interact with its environment. We will discover how this aspect of life allows for emergent behaviours to approach something closer to intelligence, and why that occurs.
